# Popup Guard privacy policy

Updated November 13, 2026.

Popup Guard is maintained at https://github.com/shrkawy/popup-guard.

## Data used by the extension

Popup Guard inspects page URLs, attempted popup destinations, navigation events, and link interactions to decide whether to allow or block new tabs. Its in-page scripts inspect links and overlays to detect unwanted click traps. Processing runs inside your browser.

The extension keeps local blocked-popup counters, a recent activity list capped at 30 entries, and domain strike counts used to learn unwanted popup destinations. Recent activity may contain page URLs, destination URLs, and timestamps. This data is not uploaded to the developer.

## Settings and Chrome Sync

Preferences, site exceptions, trusted domains, and the learned domain blocklist use Chrome's sync storage. If your browser enables extension-data synchronization, Google or your browser provider may synchronize those settings across your signed-in browsers under its own policies. Activity logs and domain strike counts use local storage rather than sync storage.

The extension has no analytics, advertisements, account system, or developer-operated server. It does not sell data or send browsing activity to the developer or advertising providers.

## Control and contact

Use Settings to pause protection, change site exceptions, manage trusted and blocked domains, disable learning, or clear activity. Uninstalling removes the extension from that browser. Your browser controls any synchronized data.

For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/shrkawy/popup-guard/issues. GitHub issues are public, so do not include private browsing details.
